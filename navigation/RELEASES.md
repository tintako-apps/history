# Navigation: all releases

## [Navigation 1.1](https://github.com/tintako-apps/history/releases/tag/navigation-v1.1)
Published 2026-11-18 · first released in [navigation/releases/v1.1](https://github.com/tintako-apps/navigation/releases/tag/v1.1)

Files: `Navigation-1.1-1-start.png`, `Navigation-1.1-2-drive.png`, `Navigation-1.1-3-map.png`, `Navigation-1.1.apk`

Offline maps and turn-by-turn navigation made for E Ink: one still picture per turn, black and white, and nothing running in the background unless you ask for it.

**What's new**
- Find a map by name: the magnifier in Add map searches every country and region, also without internet.
- Add map lists only maps you have not downloaded; your maps stay in their own list.
- Download progress moves in steps of 10 %, so the screen refreshes less.

**Fixed**
- Search results now use the whole screen.

## [Navigation 1.0](https://github.com/tintako-apps/history/releases/tag/navigation-v1.0)
Published 2026-11-17 · first released in [navigation/releases/v1.0](https://github.com/tintako-apps/navigation/releases/tag/v1.0)

Files: `Navigation-1.0-1-start.png`, `Navigation-1.0-2-drive.png`, `Navigation-1.0-3-map.png`, `Navigation-1.0.apk`

Offline maps and turn-by-turn navigation made for E Ink: one still picture per turn, black and white, and nothing running in the background unless you ask for it.

**What's new**
- First release.
- Search, routes and voice guidance without internet, by car, on foot or by bicycle.
- One clear picture per turn, your speed and the speed limit.
- Saved places, and Radio controls while driving.
- 10 languages.
