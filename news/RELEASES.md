- A card for Tintako Launcher shows your three newest headlines and a refresh key; tap a headline to read it.

- The app's name is shown in your language again (Vijesti, Nachrichten, Actualités…).

**Permissions:** unchanged (Internet, Camera). No new servers.

## [News 1.12](https://github.com/tintako-apps/history/releases/tag/news-v1.12)

News is a clean, ad-free RSS reader made for E Ink screens. It keeps only the article itself, needs no account and doesn't use Google services.

## [News 1.9](https://github.com/tintako-apps/history/releases/tag/news-v1.9)
Published 2026-11-04 · first released in [news/releases/v1.9](https://github.com/tintako-apps/news/releases/tag/v1.9)

Files: `News-1.9.apk`

A calm news reader, designed for E Ink. You choose the sources, and there is no account and no feed algorithm.

## What it can do

- **Today:** the newest stories from all the sources you follow
- **Following:** manage your sources
- **Search:** find a source by name or site address, including popular sources by country
- **Feed finder:** type a site address and the app looks for its RSS feed
- **QR scan:** send a link from your computer by scanning it
- **Clean reader:** articles without the clutter around them
- **Reading options:** text size, Lato or serif font, pictures
- **Saved:** keep stories for offline reading
- **Offline:** shows when the news was last updated

**Languages:** English, Croatian, Spanish

## Permissions
- Internet: to fetch news
- Camera: only to scan a QR code, while the scanner is open
