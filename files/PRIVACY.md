# Files: Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026.

## The short version

Files is a file manager made for E Ink devices. It is fully offline: it has no internet permission and connects to nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reports, and it doesn't use Google services. Tintako, the maker of Files, receives nothing.

## Who is responsible

Files is made by Tintako, Croatia, app@tintako.com. Tintako has no server for Files and receives none of your data.

## What the app does with your files

Files only shows, moves, renames and deletes the files on your phone that you choose. Touching a file hands it to the app on your phone that opens it, with read-only access to that one file.

## What the app stores, and where

- Its settings (language, order in a folder, hidden files), in the app's private storage.
- The Bin: deleted items wait 30 days in a hidden folder on the phone (`.Files-Trash`), together with a small list of where each came from. After 30 days they are removed for good the next time you open the app.

The app is left out of phone backups. Uninstalling it deletes its settings; the Bin folder stays on the phone until you delete it.

## Permissions, and what each is for

- **All files access:** to show your folders and to move, rename and delete what you choose. Files works only with the files on this phone and sends nothing anywhere.

## Who else receives anything

Nobody. Files has no internet permission.

## Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK)

Tintako does not process your personal data, because it receives none. Everything Files does happens on your own phone, at your request.

## Your rights

You decide what is on the phone. Tintako holds none of your data, so there is nothing to hand over or delete. You can complain to your national data protection authority (in Croatia, AZOP).

## Children

Files is suitable for everyone and collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Changes

This policy may change with a new version of the app. The version in the app (Settings → About → Privacy) says the same.
