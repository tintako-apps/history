# Tintako Launcher: all releases

## [Tintako Launcher 1.0-beta4](https://github.com/tintako-apps/history/releases/tag/launcher-v1.0-beta4) (pre-release)
Published 2026-11-20 · first released in [launcher/releases/v1.0-beta4](https://github.com/tintako-apps/launcher/releases/tag/v1.0-beta4)

Files: `TintakoLauncher-1.0-beta4.apk`

Fourth test version of Tintako Launcher, a calm home screen for E Ink phones such as the Mudita Kompakt. Works fully offline.

## What's new since 1.0-beta3
- Settings → Grid and widgets: a switch for every widget, like the switches for apps
- App icons stay sharp: an icon drawn small for a widget corner is no longer reused in the grid, and icons are drawn again after an app update
- A widget card shows the app's own sign without frame in its corner when the app provides one (for example Clock)
- An empty widget card with its own title is no longer cut off
- An app with an older widget library is shown as one widget, as it should be

## Install
Download `TintakoLauncher-1.0-beta4.apk` and open it on the phone. It installs over earlier betas and keeps your layout.

## [Tintako Launcher 1.0-beta3](https://github.com/tintako-apps/history/releases/tag/launcher-v1.0-beta3) (pre-release)
Published 2026-11-20 · first released in [launcher/releases/v1.0-beta3](https://github.com/tintako-apps/launcher/releases/tag/v1.0-beta3)

Files: `TintakoLauncher-1.0-beta3-1-start.png`, `TintakoLauncher-1.0-beta3-2-widgets.png`, `TintakoLauncher-1.0-beta3-3-apps.png`, `TintakoLauncher-1.0-beta3.apk`

Third test version of Tintako Launcher, a calm home screen for E Ink phones such as the Mudita Kompakt. Works fully offline.

## What's new since 1.0-beta1
- Larger choices in Settings, one option below the other
- A larger app menu in the middle of the screen, with Cancel
- Parental control: locked apps disappear from the launcher; Settings, App info and Uninstall ask for the phone's PIN or an own PIN
- Clear notifications in the app menu; notification badges are round and black
- A one-time offer of notification access after the launcher becomes the home screen
- Smooth shades (dithering) for imported photos, with a switch on the Wallpapers page
- Widgets appear at full size at once; quick tiles moved to the top of the Widgets page
- Support for several widgets per app (widget format card 1.1), with the time in other cities worked out on the phone
- Contacts left out, as in Mudita's own launcher
- The eye for hidden apps is gone: hide apps with their switch in Settings
- Widget keys can only be pressed by Tintako Launcher (security fix)

## [Tintako Launcher 1.0-beta1](https://github.com/tintako-apps/history/releases/tag/launcher-v1.0-beta1) (pre-release)
Published 2026-11-20 · first released in [launcher/releases/v1.0-beta1](https://github.com/tintako-apps/launcher/releases/tag/v1.0-beta1)

Files: `TintakoLauncher-1.0-beta1-1-start.png`, `TintakoLauncher-1.0-beta1-2-widgets.png`, `TintakoLauncher-1.0-beta1-3-apps.png`, `TintakoLauncher-1.0-beta1.apk`

A calm, fully offline home screen made for E Ink devices: a big clock, your apps in a clean grid, and widgets from your own apps. Black and white, no animations. This is the first beta.

**What's new**
- Three pages: widgets on the left, home in the middle, all apps on the right
- Home with a large clock and date (three positions, three sizes) and a bottom row of up to 4 apps, also shown under the app grid
- All apps in a grid that fills the screen, 3 or 4 columns, with no gaps; long press to rearrange
- Hide any app with a switch in Settings; an optional eye shows hidden apps for a quick look
- Notification badges on icons, as a number or a dot (only counted, never read)
- Widgets: Radio with play, previous, next and cast keys, and the weather from the phone's Weather app; widgets from other Tintako apps appear by themselves once those apps support them
- Quick tiles for Settings, the flashlight, airplane mode and the hotspot
- Wallpapers: built-in ones or your own picture, turned into grey tones
- 10 languages
