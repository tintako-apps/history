A simple offline e-reader made for E Ink. Copy books from your computer and they appear in the library on their own: no account, no store, no internet.

**Please read before updating:** from this version Books is signed with its own release key, so Android can't install it over 0.8 or older. Do this once:
1. In your current Books, go to Settings → General and tap **Export backup**.
2. Uninstall Books.
3. Install Books-0.8.1.apk, choose your book folder again, then tap **Import backup** in Settings → General.

Your books stay in their folder; the backup brings back your progress and bookmarks.

**Fixed**
- Books is now signed with its own release key instead of a development key, so it can be published in app stores. Features are the same as in 0.8.

## [Books 0.5](https://github.com/tintako-apps/history/releases/tag/books-v0.5)
Published 2026-11-04 · first released in tintako-apps/books as v0.5

Files: `Books-0.5.apk`

An offline e-book reader, designed for E Ink. Copy your books from your computer and they appear in your library.

## What it can do

**Library**
- Reads EPUB, PDF and comics
- Finds new books in the folder you choose
- Search by title or author
- Sort by last read, title or author
- Mark books as read or unread
- Remove books from the library and restore them; the file stays on the phone

**Reading**
- Chapters, contents and bookmarks
- Text size, font, line spacing, margins and alignment
- For PDF and comics: fit to width or whole page, and crop white margins
- Book details and reading progress

**Backup**
- Export and import reading progress, bookmarks and the book list, without the books themselves

**Languages:** English, Croatian, Spanish

## Good to know
- Books protected by DRM are not supported
- Kindle files must be converted to EPUB on a computer
- The app contains no books and isn't connected with any shop or publisher

## Privacy
Fully offline: the app has no internet permission and asks for no phone permissions.
