# Clock Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026. Applies to Clock 1.0 and later (package `com.tintako.clock`).

## The short version
Clock works entirely on your device. It has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics, and it never sends anything anywhere.

## Who is responsible
Tintako, Croatia. Contact: app@tintako.com.

## What the app stores, and where
Only on your device, in the app's private storage: your alarms, saved and running timers, the stopwatch and its laps, the cities on your world clock and your settings. Nothing is copied to backups or moved to a new device by the app. Uninstalling the app removes it all.

## Permissions, and what each is for
- **Exact alarms** (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`): so alarms and timers ring at the exact minute.
- **Run at startup** (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`): only to set your alarms again after the phone restarts, the app is updated or the time or time zone changes.
- **Over the lock screen** (`USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT`): to show the ringing screen when the phone is locked.
- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): to show a running timer and a ringing alarm with Snooze and Dismiss.
- **Foreground service and keeping the phone awake** (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`, `WAKE_LOCK`): only while an alarm or a timer is ringing, at most 10 minutes.
- **Vibration** (`VIBRATE`): to vibrate while ringing, if it is turned on.
- The Android system adds one internal permission (`DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`) that only protects the app's own internal messages.

Alarm sounds and ringtones are played from the phone itself; the app needs no permission for them.

## Widgets
The Clock widgets show your alarms, timers, the stopwatch and cities only on Tintako Launcher, on this phone. Only Tintako Launcher signed by Tintako can read them or press their keys.

## What leaves, and only when you ask
Nothing. When another app asks Clock to set an alarm or a timer, it stays on your phone. The Tip jar page shows a QR code and the address `ko-fi.com/tintako`; the app itself never opens it.

## Who else receives anything
No one. The app connects to no servers.

## Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK)
The app processes no personal data on Tintako's behalf. What it keeps stays on your device under your control.

## Your rights
Because Tintako receives no data from the app, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You can delete everything by uninstalling the app. You can complain to your national data protection authority (in Croatia, AZOP).

## Children
Clock is suitable for everyone. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version is published here and in the app (Settings → About → Privacy), with a new date.
