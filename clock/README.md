# Clock

> **No longer developed.** Clock 1.0 is the last version. All versions are kept here in History: [releases](RELEASES.md).

A calm, fully offline clock and alarm app made for E Ink devices: world clock, alarms, stopwatch and timers on big flip tiles. Black and white, no animations.

[![ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/img/githubbutton_sm.svg)](https://ko-fi.com/tintako)

## What it can do
- Alarms that ring on time, also over the lock screen, with Snooze and Dismiss on the screen, in the notification or with the volume keys
- Repeat on chosen days or ring once; skip the next ring once; name and sound for each alarm
- Sounds: the phone's alarm sounds, the phone's ringtones and three built-in tones; gradually louder and vibration if you like
- World clock with built-in cities: the day and the difference to your time at a glance, no internet needed
- Stopwatch with laps
- Timers: saved timers start with one tap, several can run at once; a running timer shows when it ends instead of counting down
- Can replace the phone's clock: other apps and voice requests can set alarms and timers in Clock
- Widgets for Tintako Launcher
- Times and dates follow the phone's 12- or 24-hour setting and region

**Languages:** Croatian, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Czech

## Permissions
- **Exact alarms:** so alarms and timers ring at the exact minute
- **Run at startup:** only to set your alarms again after the phone restarts, the app is updated or the time or time zone changes
- **Full-screen display:** to show the ringing screen when the phone is locked
- **Notifications:** to show a running timer and a ringing alarm
- **Foreground service and keep awake:** only while an alarm or a timer is ringing, at most 10 minutes
- **Vibration:** to vibrate while ringing, if turned on

No internet permission: Clock never connects to anything. Details: [Privacy](PRIVACY.md) · [Terms of use](TERMS.md) · [Licenses](LICENSES.md)
