# Clock: all releases

## [Clock 1.0](https://github.com/tintako-apps/history/releases/tag/clock-v1.0)
Published 2026-11-20 · first released in [clock/releases/v1.0](https://github.com/tintako-apps/clock/releases/tag/v1.0)

Files: `Clock-1.0-1-start.png`, `Clock-1.0-2-alarm.png`, `Clock-1.0-3-world.png`, `Clock-1.0.apk`

A calm, fully offline clock and alarm app made for E Ink devices: world clock, alarms, stopwatch and timers on big flip tiles. Black and white, no animations.

**What's new**
- First version
- Alarms that ring on time, also over the lock screen, with Snooze and Dismiss on the screen, in the notification or with the volume keys
- Repeat on chosen days or ring once, skip the next ring once, a name and a sound for each alarm
- Sounds: the phone's alarm sounds, the phone's ringtones and three built-in tones, gradually louder and with vibration if you like
- World clock with built-in cities: the day and the difference to your time at a glance
- Stopwatch with laps
- Timers: saved timers start with one tap, several can run at once, and a running timer shows when it ends
- Other apps and voice requests can set alarms and timers in Clock
- Four widgets for Tintako Launcher: alarms, timer, stopwatch and world clock
- Ten languages
