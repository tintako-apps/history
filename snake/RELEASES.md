## [Snake 1.0](https://github.com/tintako-apps/history/releases/tag/snake-v1.0)
Published 2026-11-10 · first released in [snake/releases/v1.0](https://github.com/tintako-apps/snake/releases/tag/v1.0)

Files: `Snake-1.0.apk`

A nostalgic retro game: the classic snake, made for E Ink. Fully offline, with no score, timer or streaks, and no permissions.

[![ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/img/githubbutton_sm.svg)](https://ko-fi.com/J4V82A4UVG)

**What's new**
- First release.
- Four ways to play: Slow, Medium, Fast, and Step by step, where the snake moves one cell each time you tap a direction.
- Tap the field above, below, left or right of the centre to turn, or swipe.
- The snake waits for your first tap, also after a pause.
- Hitting a wall ends the game, or switch on "Pass through walls" in Settings.
- Each way of playing keeps its own game. It pauses and saves itself when you leave or the screen turns off.
- Ten languages: Croatian, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish and Czech.
- Pure black and white, no animation, no internet permission and no Google services.
