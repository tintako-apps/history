# Calendar

> **No longer developed.** Calendar 1.0 is the last version. All versions are kept here in History: [releases](RELEASES.md).

A quiet calendar made for E Ink: your iCloud and Zoho calendars, calendars by link and a calendar on the phone, in day, month and list views.

[![ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/img/githubbutton_sm.svg)](https://ko-fi.com/tintako)

## What it can do
- Day, Month and List (the next 30 days) at the bottom of the screen
- Sync iCloud and Zoho calendars both ways
- Add view-only calendars by .ics link (Google, Outlook, Proton), typed or scanned from a QR code
- A calendar on the phone, with no sync
- Add, edit and delete events, with a place, a note and repeats; for a repeating event, change only one date or all of them
- Search titles, places and notes of the events on the phone, a year back and a year ahead
- Events already downloaded work offline
- Choose the start view, the first day of the week, where new events are saved and which calendars show

**Languages:** Croatian, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Czech

## Good to know
- iCloud, and Zoho with two-factor sign-in, need an app-specific password instead of your normal one
- No notifications and nothing running in the background; calendars refresh when you open the app
- Nothing you were typing is kept if the phone closes the app in the background

## Permissions
- Internet: to reach your calendar providers and calendar links
- Camera: only to scan a calendar link from a QR code, while the scanner is open; no picture is saved

## Privacy
No accounts with us, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md), [TERMS.md](TERMS.md) and [LICENSES.md](LICENSES.md).
