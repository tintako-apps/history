# Calendar: Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026.

## The short version

Calendar is a calendar app made for E Ink devices. It has no accounts with us, no ads, no analytics and no crash reports, and it doesn't use Google services. Everything it keeps stays on your phone. It connects only to the calendar providers and calendar links you add yourself.

## Who is responsible

Calendar is made by Tintako, Croatia, app@tintako.com. Tintako has no server for Calendar and receives none of your data.

## What the app stores, and where

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

- the addresses of the accounts you add, and the names of their calendars;
- passwords, encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore;
- the events downloaded at the last refresh;
- events you create in the phone's own calendar;
- your settings.

The app is left out of phone backups, so none of this is copied to a cloud. Remove an account to delete its events and password from the phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything, the phone's own calendar included, for good.

## Permissions, and what each is for

- **Internet:** to connect to your calendar providers and links, only while you use the app.
- **Camera:** only while you scan a calendar link from a QR code; asked the first time you open the scanner. Images are never saved or sent.

## What leaves, and only when you ask

- When you add an account, open the app or refresh, Calendar connects to your provider's calendar (CalDAV) server with the details you entered.
- When you create, change or delete an event in iCloud or Zoho, that change is sent to your provider.
- When you add or refresh a calendar by link, Calendar downloads that calendar file.
- Search runs only on the phone; nothing is sent.
- Nothing runs in the background: when the app leaves the screen, all network work stops.

## Who else receives anything

Only the services you add yourself. Each sees your IP address and handles your data under its own privacy policy:

| Service | When | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| iCloud (`caldav.icloud.com`) | if you add an iCloud account | your sign-in and the events you create, change or delete |
| Zoho (`calendar.zoho.com` or `calendar.zoho.eu`) | if you add a Zoho account | as above |
| The address of a calendar link you add | when you add or refresh it | only the request for the calendar file |

Help pages (Apple and Zoho pages for app passwords, Google, Outlook and Proton pages for calendar links) and the tip jar (Ko-fi) are shown only as QR codes to open on another device; the app never opens or contacts them itself.

## Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK)

Tintako does not process your personal data, because it receives none. Everything Calendar does with your data happens on your own phone, at your request, to give you the service you asked for. Your calendar providers process your data as controllers under their own terms.

## Your rights

You decide what is on the phone: you can delete any account or event at any time, or remove the app. Tintako holds none of your data, so there is nothing to hand over or delete. You can complain to your national data protection authority (in Croatia, AZOP).

## Children

Calendar is suitable for everyone and collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Changes

This policy may change with a new version of the app. The version in the app (Settings → About → Privacy) and this page always say the same.
