# Calendar: all releases

## [Calendar 1.0](https://github.com/tintako-apps/history/releases/tag/calendar-v1.0)
Published 2026-11-17 · first released in [calendar/releases/v1.0](https://github.com/tintako-apps/calendar/releases/tag/v1.0)

Files: `Calendar-1.0-1-start.png`, `Calendar-1.0-2-edit.png`, `Calendar-1.0-3-month.png`, `Calendar-1.0.apk`

A quiet calendar made for E Ink: your iCloud and Zoho calendars, calendars by link and a calendar on the phone, in day, month and list views.

**What's new**
- First version
- Day, Month and List (the next 30 days) at the bottom of the screen
- iCloud and Zoho calendars, both ways; Google, Outlook and Proton calendars by link, view-only
- A calendar on the phone, with no sync
- Add, edit and delete events, with a place, a note and repeats; change one date of a series or all of them
- Search the events on the phone
- Works offline with events already downloaded
- 10 languages
